Our Story

Founded in 2018, Makadara Healthcare Centre has grown from a small clinic into a Level 3A hospital trusted by over 12,500 patients annually. We combine modern technology with compassionate care.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable, and accessible healthcare to every Kenyan.

👁️ Our Vision

To be the leading community hospital in Athi River — trusted for excellence.

❤️ Compassionate Care

We treat every patient like family — with dignity and respect.

Strict hygiene, qualified staff, and SHA certified facility.

Free medical camps and health education for Athi River.
